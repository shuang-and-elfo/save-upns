# UPNS Preschool — Asset & Illustration Checklist

This document lists **every photograph and SVG line illustration** required for the redesigned University Parents Nursery School (UPNS) website. It includes suggested filenames, dimensions/aspect ratios, usage locations, alt text guidance, and family privacy/consent requirements.

---

## 1. Photography Policy & Consent Guidance

- **Parent/Family Consent Required:** Assume every photograph of a child requires explicit school approval and signed family consent.
- **Activity Over Identity:** Describe the activity in alt text rather than the child’s identity (e.g., *"Two young children building a wooden block tower in the Kitten Room"*, **never** *"Student Sarah and John playing"*).
- **No Student Names:** Never include children's names in filenames, captions, or alt attributes.
- **Easy Replacement:** All image references are centralized in `src/content/` and loaded from `public/images/` so future parent volunteers can swap images without modifying layout code.
- **Placeholder Framework:** The site includes a custom SVG/CSS photo-cutout placeholder framework with authentic Kootingal-inspired paper tape, blob masks, and handwritten labels so pages look beautiful and intentional even while waiting for final photography.
